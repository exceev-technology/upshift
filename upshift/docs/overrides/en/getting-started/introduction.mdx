**Your whole business, on one simple platform.** Upshift digitizes your company's sales and operations processes, whatever your industry. Configured around your business, hosted where you decide, live in weeks.

## Your processes, end to end

In many companies, sales and follow-ups live in Excel and WhatsApp, orders and after-sales service in separate tools. The same information is entered several times, and when someone leaves, their history leaves with them.

Upshift brings the whole chain onto one platform, with one shared record at every step:

1. **Prospecting and CRM**
2. **Quotes and orders**
3. **Purchasing and stock**
4. **Projects and installations**
5. **After-sales service and maintenance**
6. **Dashboards and reporting**

Every piece of information is entered once, then reused everywhere.

Upshift is for companies that want to run their whole business in one place, from small businesses to SMEs with several entities. Templates exist for distribution, services, installation and maintenance, and every workspace is configured around your own processes.
